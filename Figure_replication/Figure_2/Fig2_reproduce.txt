Reproducing without the videos (any computer)
---------------------------------------------
Needs only Python with numpy, matplotlib and opencv-python (cv2 is used to read the PNGs,
no video is opened), plus this folder. data_dir / layout are not used.

python generate_Fig2A.py --frames data\Fig2A_frames --dt 3 --thresh 10 --sat 40 --gain 1 --view merged --size 3.56 3.147 --margins 0.36 0.5 0.1 0.354 0.03 --bar_offset 0.05 --axis_label_offset 0.27 --axis_label_offset_bottom 0.264 --dpi 300 --also_png --output Fig2A.svg

Doses, wells and time points come from data\Fig2A_frames\manifest.csv (printed on load).
The frame pairs are for dt = 3 s, so --dt must be 3 (the script stops otherwise); the image
settings (--thresh, --sat, --gain, --view, layout and font options) can still be changed.
The PNG and SVG drawn from these frames were checked against the video-based ones: PNG
pixels are identical and the SVG is identical apart from its date/ids.

Well selection
--------------
Rule: for each dose, the well whose ActValS at 90 min (video0003) is closest to the mean
ActValS of all wells at that dose (script default: --select mean-activity --select_time 90).

  Dose        mean ActValS   rule's pick   used (--wells)
  0 mM          4259.0       C10           C10
  0.0125 mM     3700.5       E9            E9
  0.025 mM      1155.8       D11           D11
  0.1 mM          24.7       F5 (30.9)     E6 (17.3)

0.1 mM: F5 was replaced by the next-closest well, E6, because F5 has a crack (visible in all
panels). The script does not exclude wells automatically, so E6 is passed explicitly with
--wells. Without --wells the script would pick F5 for 0.1 mM.


Image processing (per panel)
----------------------------
1. Two frames from the same video: frame 0 (T0) and T0 + dt (dt = 3 s = 15 frames at 5 fps).
2. Both frames converted to grayscale by averaging the color channels, cropped to a square
   around the well (half-width = ROI radius, --pad 1.0). No other filtering or resampling
   (images drawn with nearest-neighbour interpolation).
3. Intensity-change map = T0 - T1 (gray levels). |change| < 10 (--thresh) is set to 0 (noise removal).
4. Color coding: black at 0; linear ramp to pure red (T0 brighter) / pure blue (later frame
   brighter) at |change| = 40 (--sat); fully saturated beyond that.
5. Merge: the map's brightness is the per-pixel opacity over the raw base image, where the
   base is the mean of the two frames (--gain 1, no brightness scaling). Unchanged pixels
   therefore show the raw gray image.
6. Color bar: symmetric and shared by all 16 panels, spanning +/- the largest |change| in
   the figure (-84 to 84; difference range across panels was -84 to 79). Black region = change
   below the noise threshold; colors are saturated from 40 to the ends.

Caption note: the color bar applies to the intensity-change map only; in the merged panels
unchanged pixels show the raw grayscale image rather than black.


Parameters (all as passed above)
--------------------------------
--dt 3               seconds between T0 and T1
--thresh 10          noise threshold (gray levels)
--sat 40             saturation point (gray levels)
--gain 1             base image brightness (1 = as acquired)
--view merged        (map = difference map only, raw = single frame)
--size 3.56 3.147    width x height in inches
--margins            left right top bottom gap (inches)
--bar_offset         grid -> color bar (inches)
--axis_label_offset  grid left edge -> "LEV dose (mM)" label (inches)
--axis_label_offset_bottom  grid bottom edge -> "Time (min)" label (inches)
