Figure 3 - levamisole activity time courses of the CeDiv strains (3 doses x 12 strains, 3 trials)
================================================================================================

Output: one file per panel, each 2.37 in wide x 3.0 in tall (fixed page size; 170.64 x 216 pt), Arial 10 pt,
  same y range in all three panels:
  Fig3A (0 mM), Fig3B (0.0125 mM), Fig3C (0.025 mM)  (.svg + .png, 300 dpi)
  The strain legend sits inside Fig3C (upper right, 8 pt, over the empty area above the low treated curves).
Script: generate_Fig3.py.  data/Fig3_data.csv = per-trial strain means behind the plot.

Data: ActValS (activity_by_time.py --by_condition --export_csv) of the three official trials only
  10102026_CeDiv_Leva_test02 (10 strains, no EG4725 / JU258), 10212026_CeDiv_Leva_test03, 10312026_CeDiv_Leva_test04
  base: C:\Users\jl200\Dropbox\JHU_2026_spring\Multiwell_swim\Data_Backup\My_Passport_6TB_Andersen_Lab_1
  folders control = 0 mM, p0125 = 0.0125 mM, p025 = 0.025 mM. test01 and other trials are preliminary and not used.
  Wells censored in the per-trial plots are already absent from those CSVs and stay excluded.

Statistic: per trial, strain and time point, mean ActValS of the strain's wells (5-8 per trial).
Point = mean of those trial means, error bar = sample SD (ddof = 1) across trials.
EG4725 and JU258: 2 trials; all other strains: 3 trials.
Time points are matched by index (17 per trial, ~30 min); x = mean elapsed time of that index
over the trials (trial times differ by <= ~10 min at the end of the p025 session).

Command (run from this folder; default Python 3.8 with pandas + matplotlib; Arial must be installed):
python generate_Fig3.py --base_dir "C:\Users\jl200\Dropbox\JHU_2026_spring\Multiwell_swim\Data_Backup\My_Passport_6TB_Andersen_Lab_1" --export_data data\Fig3_data.csv --size 2.37 3.0 --dpi 300 --also_png --output_prefix Fig3

Redraw without the trial folders:
python generate_Fig3.py --data data\Fig3_data.csv --size 2.37 3.0 --dpi 300 --also_png --output_prefix Fig3
