Figure 4 - curve traits of the CeDiv strains across levamisole dose (23 traits x 12 strains x 3 doses, 3 trials)
==============================================================================================================

Output: Fig4.svg (+ Fig4.png, 300 dpi); 4 panels per row, one panel per trait (order as in the H2 plots), legend in the free slot.
Script: generate_Fig4.py.  data/Fig4_data.csv = per-trial strain means (trial, strain, dose_mM, trait, value, n_wells) behind the plot.

Data: curve_traits_ActValS_per_well.csv (Ce_div_set_analysis\curve_traits.py) of the three official trials
  10102026_CeDiv_Leva_test02 (10 strains, no EG4725 / JU258), 10212026_CeDiv_Leva_test03, 10312026_CeDiv_Leva_test04
  base: C:\Users\jl200\Dropbox\JHU_2026_spring\Multiwell_swim\Data_Backup\My_Passport_6TB_Andersen_Lab_1

Statistic (as Fig 3): per trial, strain, dose and trait, mean of the strain's wells (NaN values skipped).
Point = mean of those trial means, error bar = sample SD (ddof = 1) across trials.
EG4725 and JU258: 2 trials; all other strains: 3 trials.
Traits normalized to the strain's own 0 mM control (*_delta, *_ratio, range_*, slope_rec_*, t_p*_*) are excluded at 0 mM
(same rule as the H2 analysis), so they show the two treated doses only.

Command (run from this folder; pandas + matplotlib; Arial must be installed):
python generate_Fig4.py --base_dir "C:\Users\jl200\Dropbox\JHU_2026_spring\Multiwell_swim\Data_Backup\My_Passport_6TB_Andersen_Lab_1" --export_data data\Fig4_data.csv --dpi 300 --also_png --output_prefix Fig4

Redraw without the trial folders:
python generate_Fig4.py --data data\Fig4_data.csv --dpi 300 --also_png --output_prefix Fig4

Subset of traits for the main figure, e.g.:
python generate_Fig4.py --data data\Fig4_data.csv --traits auc auc_norm A0_ratio range_ratio --ncols 2
